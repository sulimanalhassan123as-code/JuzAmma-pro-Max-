Privacy Policy

Naba Quran (Juz Amma Pro) · Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Naba Quran is a free Islamic app that helps Muslims read, learn and practise their faith: Quran reading and listening, memorization practice, prayer times, Qibla direction, dhikr, duas and community study rooms. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum the features require.

Data stored on your device

Reading progress, memorization lines, tasbih counts, prayer streaks and theme preferences are stored locally on your phone. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it.

Location

Prayer times and the Qibla compass need your location. It is used only to calculate times and direction on your device. We do not store your location on any server and we do not track your movements.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you start a recitation or tajweed review, or when you speak in a live study room. Recordings for recitation checking are sent to a speech-recognition service for scoring and are not stored permanently. Live study room audio is relayed to the room's participants while the session is active and is not kept afterwards.

AI features

The Islamic AI Guide answers your questions through a secure AI service. Questions are processed to generate a reply and are not linked to your identity. Replies may be inaccurate; always verify religious rulings with a qualified scholar.

Community study rooms

If you join or create a study room, the display name you choose, room messages, voice notes and participation records are stored so that other members can see and use the room. Do not share personal information you do not want other members to see.

Notifications

If you enable them, we send prayer-time reminders, a daily ayah and announcements. Notification delivery requires a device messaging token, which is stored only to deliver those notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Third-party services

  • Quran recitation audio is streamed from public Islamic audio sources.
  • Prayer time calculations use a public prayer-times service.
  • Speech recognition and AI answers are processed by secure third-party AI services.
  • Notification delivery uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We do not sell your data, do not show advertisements, and do not require an account for the core features.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

You can remove locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of study room messages or a messaging token linked to you, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app improves. Any change will be published on this page with a new date.
