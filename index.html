How to Perform Wudu

The Prophetic Method ﷺ · Based on Sahih al-Bukhari & Muslim

📖 Quranic Foundation
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا قُمْتُمْ إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ فَاغْسِلُوا وُجُوهَكُمْ وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ إِلَى الْمَرَافِقِ وَامْسَحُوا بِرُءُوسِكُمْ وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ إِلَى الْكَعْبَيْنِ
"O you who believe! When you intend to offer the prayer, wash your faces and your hands (forearms) up to the elbows, rub (by passing wet hands over) your heads, and (wash) your feet up to ankles."
— Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6
✅ The 6 Obligatory Acts (Fard)
These are the pillars — wudu is invalid without any of them. (Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymeen, Ar-Rawd al-Murbi')
🧼1
Wash the Face
From hairline to chin, ear to ear. Includes rinsing mouth and nose.
💪2
Wash the Arms to the Elbows
Both arms including the elbows — right arm first, then left.
🧠3
Wipe the Head
With wet hands — the amount differs by school (see below).
🦶4
Wash the Feet to the Ankles
Both feet including the ankles — right foot first, then left.
🔢5
Sequence (Tartib)
Follow the order: face → arms → head → feet. Required by Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali. Recommended by Hanafi.
⏱️6
Continuity (Muwalah)
Wash each part before the previous one dries — no long gaps between steps.

🌟 The Prophetic Method ﷺ (Most Authentic)
Based on the Hadith of Uthman ibn Affan (RA) — Sahih al-Bukhari 1934 & Sahih Muslim 226. Ibn Shihab said: "This is the most complete way of performing wudu."
💭1
Make Intention (Niyyah)
Intend in your heart to perform wudu for Allah's sake. Shafi'i school also recommends verbal intention.
🗣️2
Say Bismillah
Say "Bismillah" before starting. (Imam Ahmad considered it obligatory; majority consider it Sunnah)
🖐️3
Wash Hands 3×
Wash both hands up to the wrists, three times. Use siwak/miswak if available.
👄4
Rinse Mouth 3×
Circulate water thoroughly throughout the mouth, three times. (When fasting, do so lightly)
👃5
Rinse Nose 3×
Inhale water into the nose and blow it out, three times. (When fasting, do not inhale deeply)
🧼6
Wash Face 3×
From hairline to chin, ear to ear — three times. Run wet fingers through a thick beard.
💪7
Wash Right Arm 3×, Then Left Arm 3×
From fingertips to elbow (including the elbow). Right arm three times first, then left arm three times.
🧠8
Wipe the Head Once
Wet both hands, wipe from front to back of head, then back to front (Hadith of Abdullah bin Zayd, Bukhari 185).
👂9
Wipe the Ears Once
Index fingers inside the ears, thumbs wipe the outer ears — once. (Imam Ahmad considered it obligatory; majority Sunnah)
🦶10
Wash Right Foot 3×, Then Left Foot 3×
From toes to ankle (including the ankle). Run fingers between toes. Right foot three times, then left three times.
🤲11
Recite the Du'a
"Ashhadu an la ilaha ill-Allah wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluhu. Allahummaj'alni min al-tawwabina waj'alni min al-mutatahhirina."
"I bear witness that there is no god except Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His slave and Messenger. O Allah, make me one of those who repent and make me one of those who purify themselves."

📚 Scholarly Opinions (The 4 Madhabs)
All four schools agree on the obligatory acts. They differ on some details — all are valid.
🕌 Hanafi
Wiping head: At least 1/4 of the head
Order (Tartib): Sunnah, not obligatory
Bismillah: Sunnah
Intention: In the heart
🕌 Shafi'i
Wiping head: Any portion (even one hair)
Order (Tartib): Obligatory
Bismillah: Sunnah
Intention: In the heart + recommended verbally
🕌 Maliki
Wiping head: Entire head
Order (Tartib): Obligatory
Bismillah: Sunnah
Intention: In the heart
🕌 Hanbali
Wiping head: Entire head
Order (Tartib): Obligatory
Bismillah: Obligatory (Imam Ahmad's view)
Intention: In the heart
⚠️ What Nullifies Wudu
Natural discharges (urine, stool, gas)
Deep sleep that causes loss of awareness
Loss of consciousness
Touching the private parts directly (Shafi'i & Hanbali)
Vomiting a mouthful (Hanafi & Hanbali)
Bleeding that flows from the skin (majority)
✨ Virtues of Wudu
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever performs wudu like this, then stands and prays two rak'ahs without letting his thoughts wander, his previous sins will be forgiven." — Bukhari & Muslim
The Prophet ﷺ said: "When a Muslim — or a believer — performs wudu and washes his face, every sin he committed with his eyes comes out with the water... When he washes his feet, every sin his feet walked toward comes out with the water." — Sahih Muslim 244
The Prophet ﷺ said to Bilal (RA): "You will hear the footsteps of your shoes in Paradise ahead of you — because of the extra steps you take in wudu." — Bukhari & Muslim
